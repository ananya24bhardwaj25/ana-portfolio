SESSION NOTES

What shipped

A Flask web app that predicts flight ticket fares. Trained a Random Forest Regressor on a Kaggle flight-fare dataset, engineered the date/route features, then wrapped the fitted model in a small Flask API with an HTML/Bootstrap front end and shipped it to Heroku.

  • Model: Random Forest Regressor
  • Outcome: R² score of 0.87
  • Focus: feature engineering + preprocessing
  • Use case: dynamic pricing / revenue exploration
  • Inputs: airline, source/destination, route, duration, stops, departure & arrival time, days left to departure
  • Handling: outlier trimming on fare, log-transform on skewed price distribution
  • Validation: 80/20 train-test split, cross-checked against a linear baseline
