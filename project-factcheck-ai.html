HOW IT WORKS

A claim enters the system, relevant evidence is retrieved, and that evidence is passed to the language model before a verdict is produced. In simple terms: search first, reason second.

  • Finds relevant evidence with semantic search
  • Uses FAISS for fast evidence retrieval
  • Uses Mistral through Ollama for the reasoning step
  • Returns Supported, Refuted or Unverifiable with reasoning
SYSTEM THINKING

The key idea is simple: give the model evidence before asking it to explain the claim. That makes the workflow easier to understand and audit.
