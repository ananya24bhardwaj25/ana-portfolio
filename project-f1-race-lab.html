SESSION NOTES

What it answers

A race-strategy analysis platform built on real FastF1 data, around three questions: when should a driver pit given current tyre wear, how much did a Safety Car or VSC actually cost, and is the pace gap between two drivers real or noise.

  • Tyre degradation: fuel-corrected lap time vs tyre age, compound and track temperature
  • Quantile gradient boosting gives P10 / P50 / P90 bands, not one point guess
  • SHAP values show what drives degradation
  • Safety Car cost: per-stint green-flag baseline projected through the SC period, bootstrapped for a confidence interval
  • Pit simulator: Monte Carlo over degradation curves, track-specific pit loss and Safety Car probability
  • Bundled sample data keeps the app working offline
